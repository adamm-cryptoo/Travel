Policies

Last updated 23 November 2026

Booking

The lead traveller must be 18 or older and carry valid ID. Prices are per person and confirmed at payment.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel free within 24 hours of purchase. After that, the supplier's fare rules apply. Premium members get longer free cancellation, as shown on the plans page.

Payments

We accept major cards and bank transfer. Card details go to our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to complete your trip and never sell personal data. You can ask for a copy or deletion at any time.

Traveller conduct

Respect local laws and customs. We may cancel bookings that involve illegal activity, without refund.
